# Executive Intent - Phase 0

**Date Created:** 2026-06-02  
**Created By:** Tech Lead + Project Sponsor  
**Approved By:** Kalu

## The Business Problem

Relief Hotels needs a premium digital presence that reflects a five-star brand standard and converts interest into real bookings. The current market expects hotel websites to support discovery, trust-building, and direct booking actions without heavy back-and-forth.

Our business currently risks losing high-intent guests when booking is not immediate, secure, and visible end-to-end. This is especially critical for local luxury guests, international tourists, and corporate event organizers who compare multiple properties quickly.

## Who Is Affected

- **Primary Users:** Local luxury travelers in Nigeria seeking premium stays and concierge-quality experiences
- **Secondary Users:** International tourists planning hotel stays and city/state experiences
- **Business Stakeholders:** Hotel management, reservations team, marketing, finance, and concierge operations

## Success Looks Like

- **Primary KPI:** Paid online bookings reach **20 per month** within initial rollout
- **Secondary KPIs:**
  - Fully functional website with stable uptime and responsive pages
  - Secure online booking and reservation flow available to guests
  - Reservation/booking notifications delivered to hotel manager via **SMS and/or WhatsApp**

## Strategic Alignment

This initiative supports:

- [x] Company objective to strengthen web presence and direct bookings
- [x] Revenue growth through paid online booking conversion
- [x] Service quality objective through faster reservation awareness and response

## Investment Justification

- **Estimated Investment:** ~**N2,000,000** (initial build and launch scope)
- **Expected Benefit:** More direct bookings, improved premium positioning, lower booking friction
- **Payback Assumption:** Achievable with steady monthly paid bookings and event/reservation upsell

## Critical Success Factors

1. Booking flow is secure, reliable, and easy on mobile
2. Manager notifications are dependable (SMS/WhatsApp or both)
3. Brand experience remains elegant and trustworthy for target audiences
