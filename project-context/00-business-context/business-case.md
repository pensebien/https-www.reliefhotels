# Business Case

## Problem Statement

Relief Hotels requires a stronger digital channel to represent a luxury brand and convert high-intent visitors into confirmed reservations and paid bookings.

## Why Now

- Guest expectations now include immediate online discovery and booking
- Competitors offer richer digital experiences and conversion funnels
- Delay in lead response reduces booking close rate, especially for premium travelers

## Proposed Solution

Build and operate a modern hotel platform with:

1. Elegant web presence aligned with five-star hospitality
2. Secure booking and payment-ready reservation flow
3. Room, tour, and event discovery with clear CTAs
4. Notification automation to management via SMS/WhatsApp for operational speed

## Target Segments (Priority Order)

1. **Local luxury travelers**
2. **International tourists**
3. **Corporate events**

## Expected Business Outcomes

- Reach at least **20 paid online bookings/month**
- Improve conversion from website visits to inquiry/booking actions
- Reduce manual bottlenecks and missed reservations
- Strengthen premium perception and repeat demand

## Scope Notes

- Initial budget target: **N400k** (rough estimate)
- Delivery includes website, booking flow, and reservation notification capability
- **Front-desk cashier (ADR-005):** hotelier settles existing reservations in-person via **cash, Paystack Terminal, and/or Moniepoint**, with offline-tolerant cash sync — supports conversion when guests do not complete web Paystack
- **Light HMS (post-cashier):** minibar/F&B folio, role-scoped staff views, accounting ledger, occupancy calendar — see `03-planning/hms-expansion-roadmap.md`
- Future expansion can include deeper CRM, **loyalty** (`05-monitoring-value/loyalty-todo.md`), and advanced analytics integrations
