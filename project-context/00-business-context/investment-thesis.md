# Investment Thesis

## Summary

A focused digital investment will unlock direct online bookings, reduce conversion loss, and strengthen Relief Hotels' premium market positioning.

## Initial Budget Envelope

- **Estimated Budget:** **N2,000,000** (rough estimate)

## Proposed Budget Allocation (Draft)

- Product design and UX polish: N80,000
- Engineering and integrations: N170,000
- Content/media preparation: N60,000
- QA, launch readiness, contingency: N50,000
- Notification/payment operational setup: N40,000

> Allocation can be adjusted after vendor/provider pricing confirmation.

## Value Drivers

1. More direct paid bookings from web traffic
2. Better conversion from premium audience segments
3. Faster operational response via manager notifications
4. Reduced dependency on manual-only booking channels

## Risk and Mitigation

- **Risk:** Payment/notification integration delays  
  **Mitigation:** Demo-safe fallback paths and phased provider rollout

- **Risk:** Low conversion despite traffic  
  **Mitigation:** Improve landing pages, pricing clarity, and trust signals

- **Risk:** Operational follow-up lag  
  **Mitigation:** SMS/WhatsApp automation + response-time ownership

## Economic Decision Criteria

Proceed if:

- Core platform launches with secure booking flow
- Notification loop to manager is functioning
- Monthly paid bookings trend toward 20 target within ramp period
