# Stakeholder Alignment

## Stakeholder Map

- **Executive Sponsor:** Kalu
- **Tech Lead:** Project owner (you)
- **Operations Stakeholder:** Hotel Manager / Reservations Manager
- **Supporting Stakeholders:** Concierge and Marketing support team

## Roles and Responsibilities

### Executive Sponsor (Kalu)
- Approves strategic direction, budget envelope, and phase gates
- Confirms launch-readiness from business perspective

### Tech Lead
- Owns architecture, implementation, integrations, and delivery quality
- Owns KPI instrumentation and reporting setup

### Hotel Manager / Reservations Manager
- Validates reservation handling flow
- Owns response-time SLA and escalation process

### Concierge / Marketing Support
- Supports content quality, guest messaging tone, and campaign alignment

## Decision Cadence

- **Weekly:** build/progress review
- **Bi-weekly:** KPI and conversion checkpoint
- **Monthly:** business-value review vs paid-booking target

## Alignment Risks

- Ambiguity over who responds to reservation alerts
- Changes to launch scope without explicit prioritization
- Incomplete KPI ownership

## Alignment Controls

- Single accountable owner per KPI
- Written acceptance criteria before each release phase
- Go/No-Go checklist signed by Executive Sponsor + Tech Lead
